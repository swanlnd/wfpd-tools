Set Up Your Private Budget

A step-by-step guide to using the Aspire budget sheet and the phone input form — set up once, then log spending in seconds. Everything stays in your own Google account.

🔒 Your data stays yours. You'll make your own copy of both files into your own Google Drive. Your salary and spending are never shared with L&D or anyone at work — this is a tool for you.
Heads-up: these steps assume the input Form is linked to a responses tab in the sheet, and that each person keeps their own private copy. If your setup differs, tell MZ and the steps can be adjusted.

Part 1 · Make your own copies

~3 min
1

Copy the budget sheet

Open the Aspire Budgeting sheet → It will ask "Make a copy?" — click Make a copy and it saves to your own Google Drive. This copy is now yours and private.

2

Copy the input form

Open the Aspire Budget input form → Click Make a copy to save your own version into your Drive.

Why copy, not just fill it in? If you type into the shared form, your spending would flow to the L&D account. Your own copy keeps every number in your Drive only.

Part 2 · Connect your form to your sheet

~4 min
3

Link the form to your sheet

Open your copied form → the Responses tab → click Link to Sheets → choose Select existing spreadsheet → pick your copied Aspire sheet.

A new tab called Form Responses 1 appears in your sheet — that's where entries land.

4

Pull responses into Transactions — one formula, no dragging

Open the Transactions tab, click the first empty DATE cell in the entries area, and paste this in:

={ QUERY('Form Responses 1'!$B$2:$F, "select B, C where B is not null", 0), ARRAYFORMULA(IF(QUERY('Form Responses 1'!$B$2:$B, "select B where B is not null", 0)="","","")), QUERY('Form Responses 1'!$B$2:$F, "select D, E, F where B is not null", 0) }

This fills DATE, OUTFLOW, (blank INFLOW), CATEGORY, ACCOUNT, MEMO in one go, and grows by itself as you add entries — no dragging, ever.

Match it to your form: the letters (B date, C amount, D category, E account, F memo) must match your Form Responses 1 column order, and the tab name must match exactly. If your columns sit elsewhere, just swap the letters.
5

Do a test entry

Submit one dummy expense in your form, then check it appears on the Transactions tab and shows up on the Dashboard.

Nothing showing? Check the tab name and column letters in the formula, and make sure the category/account names in your form match the Configuration tab exactly — or ask MZ.

Part 3 · Set it up for your life

~10 min
6

Set your income & categories

In your sheet, open the Configuration tab. Enter your monthly take-home pay, then rename/add categories (rent, groceries, petrol, phone, fun money, savings…) and your accounts so they match how you actually live.

7

Enter your starting balances

On the Transactions tab, add your current balance for each account so the sheet starts from reality.

8

Give every riyal a job

On Category Transfers, move your income from "available to budget" into each category until it's all assigned. Check the Dashboard to see the plan.

Part 4 · Use it every day

~30 sec
9

Put the form one tap away

Open your form on your phone → browser menu → Add to Home screen (or bookmark it). Now logging a spend is one tap.

10

Log as you spend

Bought something? Open the form, fill in date, amount, category, account, and a short note, then Submit. Takes seconds — do it on the spot so nothing's forgotten.

11

Check in weekly

Once a week, open your sheet → Dashboard to see where you stand, and Reports for the trends. Adjust categories on Category Transfers as life happens.

Handy to know

My entry isn't showing up

Check the pull formula in Transactions (Part 2, step 4) — the tab name and column letters must match your Form Responses 1 tab, and category/account names must match the Configuration tab exactly.

Keep names identical

The #1 cause of trouble is a category typed differently in the form vs the sheet. Copy-paste names to be safe.

It's your data — always

Nobody at work can see your copy. To stop using it, just delete your copies from your Drive.

Want the fancy extras?

Aspire offers an optional paid add-on for automatic bank-statement imports. Totally optional — the free manual way keeps your data most private.
