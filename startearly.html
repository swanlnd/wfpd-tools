How to read it: each bar is the fixed amount you'd set aside every month to reach your target by your chosen age, if it grew at the rate shown. Start younger and the bar shrinks dramatically — that's compounding rewarding time in the market.

About the rate: a diversified stock ETF has historically returned roughly 7% a year over the long run (before inflation) — that's the default here. More cautious planning uses ~5%, and cash or fixed deposits far less. Change it to see how sensitive the numbers are.

This is a simplified illustration assuming a steady return compounded monthly; real markets rise and fall and nothing here is guaranteed. Amounts in QAR. Educational only, not financial advice.
